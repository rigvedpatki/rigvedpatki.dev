## About Me

Hey there! I'm a software engineer who loves turning complex problems
into elegant solutions—one line of code at a time.

With 9+ years in the trenches of full-stack development, I've built
everything from intricate web dashboards to scalable cloud-native
applications. Currently, I'm at Cohesity as Member of Technical Staff
III, where I get to work on challenging problems at the intersection
of frontend architecture and backend systems. For the past two years,
I've also been wearing the Scrum Master hat, leading a team of 7
talented developers through the full development lifecycle—from sprint
planning to retrospectives.

My toolkit is diverse: Angular, React, and Vue.js on the frontend;
Node.js, NestJS, Java Spring Boot, and Go on the backend; AWS and GCP
for cloud infrastructure; and Docker and Kubernetes for keeping
everything running smoothly. But beyond the tech stack, what really
drives me is creating software that people actually enjoy using.

I'm a firm believer that great software comes from great
collaboration. Whether I'm pair programming with a colleague,
mentoring junior developers (helping them navigate their first complex
feature or debugging session), or facilitating team discussions, I
value the human side of software development as much as the technical
side. There's something incredibly fulfilling about seeing someone
you've mentored ship their first major feature with confidence.

When I'm not coding, you'll probably find me exploring new
technologies, experimenting with side projects, or thinking about
better ways to solve the problems I tackled today. I see every project
as an opportunity to learn something new and push my skills a little
further.

If you're looking for someone who combines technical depth with a
passion for building things that matter, let's connect!
